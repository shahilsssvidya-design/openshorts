# Original OpenShorts Demo Tracks

These three instrumental WAVs were generated and arranged for the OpenShorts Background Music upgrade from simple synthesized notes and drums. No third-party audio recordings or existing commercial music were sampled or bundled.

- `night-drive.wav` — cinematic / synth, 108 BPM
- `bright-bounce.wav` — playful / upbeat, 120 BPM
- `soft-focus.wav` — ambient / calm, 90 BPM

They are provided for use in OpenShorts videos, including monetized uploads, without an attribution requirement. You can also generate replacement WAVs with `build_demo_music.py` in the upgrade package (requires numpy only when regenerating).

Users are responsible for complying with platform audio policies and for ensuring that any *other* audio they upload is appropriately licensed. Automatic Content ID systems are not controlled by OpenShorts.
